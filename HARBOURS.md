# THE DEEP DIVER — HARBOURS CHART

*Every archive available, as near as "every" honestly goes. Compiled
2026-11-05 for this chart. One honest limit up front: the literal
number of archives on Earth is in the thousands — every university,
county, parish and company keeps one. This chart lists every MAJOR
open harbour in the waters the Diver hunts (government paper, books,
technical reports, newspapers, manuals, film/sound), in fleets. It is
a living chart: a harbour joins the Diver's allowlist only when its
door module is built and standards-checked (STANDARDS.md). Access
words: **Open** = documents downloadable by a plain program ·
**Mixed** = catalogue open, documents partly open · **Index-only** =
you can learn that it exists and where it sits, not take it home ·
**Locked** = lending/subscription wall — a lead, never a grab.*

## HOME WATERS — the chart re-sorts around the diver (2026-11-05)

"If a guy in India downloads it, I want his closer archives listed."
Ruled: the chart is not one fixed order. The face asks the user ONCE
for their home country — picked by the user from a list, stored on
their own machine. No GPS, no tracking, no asking twice; it is a
preference, not a location. The chart then leads with a **Home
Fleet**: that country's national archive and digital library, its
government documents shelf, its newspaper vault, its university and
state/provincial shelves, and the world collections strong in its
languages and history. The nine world fleets below follow after,
unchanged and fully reachable — home first, never home only.

Home fleets are compiled per country as the desk work is done (the
pattern is the example below); a home harbour still joins the
allowlist only through a built door and a standards check, same as
any other water.

** worked example — Home Fleet: India**
- **Abhilekh Patal** (National Archives of India) — government records portal. **Mixed**.
- **National Digital Library of India** — the national learning shelf, books and theses in Indian languages and English. **Open/Mixed**.
- **Digital Library of India** (on archive.org) — millions of Indian-language and English books scanned under the Million Books project. **Open**.
- **Shodhganga** (INFLIBNET) — Indian university PhD theses, full text. **Open**.
- **South Asia Open Archives** (JSTOR Labs) — curated South Asian historical documents and periodicals. **Open**.
- **State archives & Asiatic Society / Roja Muthiah Research Library class shelves** — regional records and Tamil print heritage; reached by referral. **Mixed**.
- Plus Fleet-7 world shelves strong in South Asian material (British Library's India Office Records class — catalogue **Index-only/Mixed**).

**The author's own Home Fleet is the USA** (Michigan state shelves first
under Fleet 9's rule) — which is why Fleets 1–6 read American-first
in this copy of the chart.

## Fleet 1 — US government & the declassified shelf

- **CIA Reading Room / CREST** (cia.gov, mirrored on archive.org) — declassified intelligence documents. **Open** — CHARTED in v1 (via the mirror).
- **National Archives — NARA Catalog** (archives.gov) — the federal record itself: documents, photos, film. **Mixed**, public API.
- **Presidential Libraries** (NARA's 15, incl. the JFK Library) — each president's papers; JFK's holds the assassination-era files. **Mixed**.
- **FBI Vault** (vault.fbi.gov) — released FBI files, FOIA reading room. **Open**.
- **NSA FOIA Reading Room** (nsa.gov) — declassified signals-intelligence documents. **Open**.
- **State Department — FOIA Reading Room + Office of the Historian / FRUS** (state.gov, history.state.gov) — diplomatic documents; FRUS is the official edited record, fully online. **Open**.
- **DIA / ODNI / NGA reading rooms** — smaller declassified shelves from the other intelligence agencies. **Open/Mixed**.
- **National Security Archive** (nsarchive.gwu.edu) — George Washington University's FOIA-driven collection; the best-curated declassified shelf outside government. **Mixed** (many full documents open, some briefings subscriber-side).
- **Digital National Security Archive (ProQuest)** — the commercial deep version of the above. **Locked**.
- **GovInfo** (govinfo.gov) — Federal Register, Congressional Record, bills, the official daily paper of government. **Open**, API.
- **Congress.gov / Library of Congress** — legislation, plus the LoC's own digital collections (below). **Open/Mixed**.
- **USA.gov / agency FOIA libraries generally** — every federal agency must keep a FOIA reading room; small shelves, same door shape. **Mixed**.

## Fleet 2 — Military, science & technical reports

- **DTIC** (dtic.mil) — defence technical reports, the military's OSTI. **Mixed** (public collection open; some distribution-limited — limited means limited).
- **NASA NTRS** (ntrs.nasa.gov) — every NASA technical paper ever published. **Open**, API.
- **DOE OSTI** (osti.gov) — energy/nuclear research reports incl. declassified Manhattan District paper. **Open**.
- **NIST Digital Library** (nist.gov) — standards, handbooks, metrology. **Open**.
- **USGS Publications Warehouse** — geological surveys, maps' papers. **Open**.
- **ERIC** (eric.ed.gov) — education research reports. **Mixed**.
- **PubMed Central** (ncbi.nlm.nih.gov) — biomedical papers, full text. **Open**.
- **arXiv** (arxiv.org) — physics/math/CS preprints. **Open**.
- **ADS — Astrophysics Data System** (ui.adsabs.harvard.edu) — astronomy literature incl. historical observatory reports. **Mixed**.
- **USPTO / Google Patents** — every US patent, drawings included — a first-class harbour for machine and instrument hunts. **Open**.
- **NRC ADAMS** (nrc.gov) — nuclear regulatory documents. **Mixed**.

## Fleet 3 — Books, texts & the great libraries

- **Internet Archive** (archive.org) — the biggest open shelf on Earth: texts, the Reading Room mirror, journals (incl. the die-cast run), software, film, sound. **Open** — CHARTED in v1.
- **HathiTrust** (hathitrust.org) — university libraries' joint scan vault; public-domain full text open, the rest search-the-text index-only. **Mixed/Index-only**, API for the open part.
- **Google Books** — the deepest book index ever made; full view only where rights allow. **Index-only** for most, **Open** for public domain.
- **Project Gutenberg** (gutenberg.org) — public-domain books, clean text. **Open**.
- **Open Library** (openlibrary.org) — archive.org's catalogue face for books; borrowing is **Locked**, metadata **Open** (API).
- **Library of Congress Digital Collections** (loc.gov) — manuscripts, photos, maps, newspapers (below). **Open/Mixed**, API.
- **Digital Public Library of America** (dp.la) — one catalogue over thousands of US libraries/archives/museums — the harbour of harbours for American material. **Index + links**, API.
- **Smithsonian Open Access** (si.edu) — museum objects, images, data. **Open**, API.
- **CORE** (core.ac.uk) — open-access research papers aggregated worldwide. **Open**, API.
- **WorldCat** (worldcat.org) — the index of indexes: which library on Earth holds a thing. **Index-only** — it answers "where," never "give."

## Fleet 4 — Newspapers, magazines & journals

- **Chronicling America** (loc.gov) — US newspapers 1777–1963, page images + OCR. **Open**, API.
- **Internet Archive magazine/journal collections** — trade press and periodicals (the die-cast journals live here). **Open**.
- **Trove** (trove.nla.gov.au) — Australia's newspapers/books vault, superb OCR. **Open**.
- **Gallica** (gallica.bnf.fr) — France's national library digital shelf: books, press, manuscripts. **Open/Mixed**.
- **Delpher** (delpher.nl) — Dutch newspapers/books/radio. **Open**.
- **Newspapers.com / GenealogyBank / ProQuest Historical** — the commercial newspaper vaults. **Locked**.
- **Google News Archive** — scanned papers, browsing mostly; the index is the value. **Index-only/Mixed**.

## Fleet 5 — Film, sound & images

- **Prelinger Archives** (on archive.org) — industrial/ephemeral film, incl. factory and trade films. **Open**.
- **Internet Archive TV News / Moving Image collections** — broadcast news recordings and more film. **Open/Mixed**.
- **WGBH Open Vault** — public broadcasting's archives. **Mixed**.
- **National Film & Sound Archive (AU), BFI (UK), CNC (FR)** — national film shelves. **Mixed/Index-only**.
- **Wikimedia Commons** — public-domain media of every kind, heavily government-sourced. **Open**, API.
- **Flickr Commons** — archives' photo streams (LoC, Smithsonian, national libraries). **Open**.

## Fleet 6 — Law & courts

- **CourtListener / RECAP** (courtlistener.com) — US federal court filings and opinions, crowd-freed from PACER. **Open**, API.
- **Google Scholar Case Law** — court opinions index. **Index-only/Mixed**.
- **Justia / Cornell LII** — opinions, codes, regulations in the open. **Open**.
- **PACER** itself — the federal courts' own counter: public but per-page priced. **Fee wall** — a lead source, priced grabs are the user's call only.

## Fleet 7 — Foreign national archives & world libraries

- **The National Archives (UK)** — Discovery catalogue + growing digital downloads. **Mixed** (some paid downloads — priced means the user's call).
- **Library and Archives Canada** — incl. digitised military and government files. **Mixed**.
- **National Archives of Australia + Trove** (above). **Mixed/Open**.
- **Europeana** (europeana.eu) — the EU's DPLA: one catalogue over Europe's libraries/museums. **Index + links**, API.
- **Deutsche Digitale Bibliothek** (deutsche-digitale-bibliothek.de) — Germany's Europeana twin. **Index + links**.
- **Polona** (polona.pl) — Poland's national digital library. **Open/Mixed**.
- **National Diet Library Digital Collections** (Japan, dl.ndl.go.jp) — **Mixed**, much view-only in-country by rights.
- **Abhilekh Patal** (abhilekh-patal.in) — National Archives of India's portal. **Mixed**.
- **DigitalNZ** (digitalnz.org) — New Zealand's DPLA. **Index + links**, API.
- **Memoria Chilena / Biblioteca Nacional Digital de Chile**, **Hemeroteca Digital (Spain, bne.es)**, **Biblioteca Digital Hispánica** — national shelves, same door shape. **Mixed**.
- Rule for the fleet: every sovereign state keeps a national archive and most now keep a digital shelf; this fleet grows by flag as hunts require. Foreign-language shelves are dived with the same standards; the desk translates judgment-side.

## Fleet 8 — Trade, industry & the machine shelves (the workshop water)

- **Internet Archive trade-journal & manual collections** — the die-cast shelf's home; also machinery, radio, electronics periodicals. **Open**.
- **Manuals collections** (archive.org manuals library; ManualsLib as index) — operator/service manuals. **Open/Index-only**.
- **Smithsonian & museum trade-literature collections** — manufacturers' catalogues and trade ephemera. **Mixed**.
- **HathiTrust trade catalogues** — the deep shelf for pre-1923 industrial catalogues. **Mixed**.
- **Google Patents + Espacenet** (European Patent Office) — Espacenet covers the world's patents. **Open**.
- Standards bodies' public shelves (**NIST** above; **SAE/ASTM** proper are **Locked**).

## Fleet 9 — The long tail (named so it is not invisible)

- **State archives & libraries** (all 50; Michigan's is the Archives of Michigan / Michiganology) — state/local records, photos, newspapers. **Mixed**.
- **University special collections & institutional repositories** — thousands; catalogued through WorldCat, DPLA, and CORE rather than one by one.
- **Historical societies, company archives, union/church/regimental collections** — found through the catalogues above; each is a harbour of one.
- These are reached by referral: a Fleet 1–8 catalogue names the shelf; the shelf gets a door if a hunt needs it twice.

## Off the chart

The forbidden waters stand as written in STANDARDS.md — dark web,
executable content, people-collections — and do not appear here,
because this is a chart of where the Diver *can* be sent.
